Turīya

The Kālī-kula tradition preserves the worship of the Bhairava known as Turīya and his consort, the goddess Turīyā. A corrupt macranthropic visualization of the Bhairava Turīya is provided in texts of this tradition, like the Hāhārāva tantra. 

The central location of Kubera in this visualization is notable and suggests a connection to his ancient association with Rudra and the Kauberī tradition. Below is a preliminary emendation of this hymn:

muni-vargā mahā-bhāgāḥ sāvadhānaṃ śṛṇuṣva me ।  
asmad vibhūtiṃ vakṣyāmi utpattiṃ prakṛteḥ svayam ॥  
O highly fortunate, assembly of sages! Listen with full attention. I shall declare my own divine manifestation, and the very origin of Prakṛti itself."

mal-lalāṭāsthito brahmā śravaṇayoḥ pañca-devatāḥ ।  
nāsāgrāt salilaṃ caiva mukhād agnir ajāyata || 1 ॥  
Brahmā resides on my forehead, and the five deities (_Pañca-devatā_) reside in the two ears. From the tip of the nose emerges water, and from the face, fire was born.

rasanāyāṃ sarasvatī devī netrābhyām amaraughajaḥ ।  
kaṇṭhe nīlā mahādevī bhuje durgārti-mardinī (durgāri-mardinī ) || 2 ॥  
On the tongue is the goddess Sarasvatī; from the two eyes is born the stream of the immortals (gods). In the throat rests the great goddess Nīlā (Nīlasarasvatī), and on the arms is Durgā, the crusher of afflictions (foes).

stana-madhye ca kandarpaḥ kukṣau sapta-samudrakāḥ ।  
kara-madhye ca devaughāḥ pāpa-puṇyau vakṣasthale || 3 ॥  
In the midst of the breasts is Kandarpa (Kāmadeva), and within the belly are the seven cosmic oceans. In the palms of the hands reside the lineage of gods, and on the chest rest both sin and virtue.

hṛdaye candramā devo nābhau ca garuḍa-dhvajaḥ ।  
vāma-pārśve ca pitaro dakṣiṇe śakra-rāṭ svayam || 4 ॥  
In the heart is the Moon god, and in the navel is Garuḍa-dhvaja (Viṣṇu). On the left flank reside the Ancestors (_Pitṛs_), and on the right is King Śakra (Indra) himself.

guhye gaṇapatir devo hy urosi kāla-mṛtyukaḥ ।  
pṛṣṭhe nakṣatra-devāś ca liṅge caiva sadāśivaḥ || 5 ॥  
In the private part is the god Gaṇapati, and on the upper chest (_uras_) is Kāla-Mṛtyu (Time-Death). On the back are the stellar deities (constellations), and on the phallus is Sadāśiva.

romeṣu mānasī devī asthīni ca sirās tathā ।  
rudhiraṃ parvatāḥ sarve jaṅghayoḥ dharma-devatā || 6 ॥  
In the hair [on the body] rests the Goddess of the Mind; the bones and the veins, as well as the blood, are all the sacred mountains. On the two shanks resides the deity of Dharma.

guda-deśe guho devaḥ ṛṣayo hy ūru-deśayoḥ ।  
daitya-rākṣasa-martyāś ca jihvāyāṃ sarva-devatāḥ || 7 ॥  
In the region of the rectum is the god Guha, and the sages reside upon the two thighs. The Daityas, Rākṣasas, and mortals – indeed all remaining deities – are located upon the tongue.

deha-madhye kubero devo vibhūtir muni-vargāḥ ।
evaṃ vibhūti-yogaṃ ca gopanīyaṃ prayatnataḥ || 8 ॥  
In the center of the body is Kubera, the mighty god, O assembly of sages! This _Vibhūti-yoga_ (Cosmic Integration) must be concealed with utmost effort.

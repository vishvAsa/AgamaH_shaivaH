> Seems like this percolated to vajrAyana and they even have a special form for vArAhi as vajraghonA

That is indeed a parallel and suggests a link to early bhairava-srotas, which is the only place a comparable name is used (barring the yoginI dIrghaghoNA in a saiddhAntika and bhairavIya prayoga) TMK. 

The "ghoratara" forms of the mAtR^i-s are sacrificed to in the bhairava-srotas fire ritual with specific mantra-s paralleling those of the shambhu-nirNaya thus:

**bhairava-srotas:**
Central:  
mahAbhairava – karAlI   
krodha-vaTuka-bhairava – mAhAyoginI-s  
kulagaNeshvara – mahAyoginI-s  

Ogdoad:

1. asitA~Nga – ghora-brahmavati
2. ruru – bhIma-rudravati
3. chaNDa – kumAravati
4. krodha – vaikuNThavati
5. unmatta – ghoNavati
6. kapAla – shakravati
7. bhIma – charchikAbhayA
8. saMhAraka – saMhAralakShmI

**shambhu-nirNaya:**
Central:
naveshvara (=navAtman) – naveshvarI

Ogdoad:
1. ma~Ngala – brAhmI
2. charchaka – mAheshvarI
3. yoga – kumArikA
4. harasiddha – vaiShNavI
5. bhaTTa – ghoNikA
6. kilikila – shakrI
7. kAlarAtra – chAmuNDA
8. bhIShaNa – kamalA

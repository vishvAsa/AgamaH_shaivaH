Posting for info only. The 8 incantations of the old mAtR^i-yAga:

oṃ hrīṃ phreṃ hāṃ āṃ asitāṅga-brahmavatyai svāhā || 1 ||  
oṃ hrīṃ phreṃ hāṃ kāṃ ruru-rudravatyai svāhā || 2 ||  
oṃ hrīṃ phreṃ hāṃ cāṃ caṇḍa-kumāravatyai svāhā || 3 ||  
oṃ hrīṃ phreṃ hāṃ ṭāṃ krodha-viṣṇuvatyai svāhā || 4 ||  
oṃ hrīṃ phreṃ hāṃ tāṃ unmatta-ghoṇavatyai svāhā || 5 ||  
oṃ hrīṃ phreṃ hāṃ pāṃ kapāla-śakravatyai svāhā || 6 ||  
oṃ hrīṃ phreṃ hāṃ yāṃ bhīma-carcikābhayavatyai svāhā || 7 ||  
oṃ hrīṃ phreṃ hāṃ śāṃ saṃhāraka-saṃhāralakṣmyai svāhā || 8 ||
